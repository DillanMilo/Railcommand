What a reviewer can inspect

Actor ID/role, exact UTC timestamp, sequence, request revision/hash, policy/cost/budget version, reason and operation ID. Corrections reference prior events; they do not replace them. Export follows the same permissions.

When this request cannot proceed

Count or budget hold

Keep the request and inputs. Resolve the count discrepancy or approve a distinct budget revision before retrying final authorization.

Stale or changed revision

Show the changed fields and ask for review. Prior approvals remain in history but do not approve new content.

Approver unavailable

Waiting state with named backup. Reassignment appends an event; there is no shared login or silent override.

Other states to design and verify

Offline: read-only previously synchronized operational status; decisions require connection. Empty queue: no assigned steps. Denied membership: no evidence response. Submit/approve timeout: look up the same operation receipt; do not create a second decision. Conflict: keep reason text, reload current revision and request explicit review.
